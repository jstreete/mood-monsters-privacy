# Mood Monsters Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

Mood Monsters stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell personal information.

## Data we handle

- Child profile names
- Incident logs (behaviors, triggers, resolutions, intensity, notes, timestamps, and related details)
- App settings and preferences

All of this data remains on your device unless you choose to export or back it up.

## No tracking

Mood Monsters does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

## Optional features

You may choose to:

- Export reports (CSV/PDF) through the iOS share sheet
- Back up data to your own Google Drive account
- Purchase a subscription through Apple In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. Mood Monsters does not receive or store your payment information.
